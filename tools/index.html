⚔️ Weapon Selection

Weapons are mainly used for exploring the mines and fighting monsters. Choosing the right weapon boosts combat efficiency.

🛡️ Recommended Weapon Path

  1. Rusty Sword (initial) → Starter weapon
  2. Steel Smallsword (early levels) → Found in a chest on Mine levels 1–5
  3. Neptune's Glaive (mid levels) → Found in a chest around Mine level 20
  4. Shadow Fang (deeper levels) → Purchased at the Adventurer's Guild
  5. Galaxy Sword (endgame) → Obtained by offering a Prismatic Shard at the Three Pillars in the desert

🎒 Backpack and Storage

Managing your backpack and storage is key to efficiency. Backpack capacity directly affects how long you can stay out and how much loot you can bring back, while a good storage system lets you find items quickly.

  • Keep frequently used tools in the hotbar (first 5–8 slots)
  • Build enough storage chests on the farm, organized by category
  • Sort items by type (materials, products, gifts, minerals, weapons)
  • Regularly tidy your backpack and stash items you don't need right now into chests

📐 Tool Upgrade Cost-Benefit Framework

Tool upgrades cost gold and ore, and the tool is unavailable for two days. To decide whether an upgrade is worth it, evaluate it on four dimensions:

Dimension Description Weight
Frequency of useHow many times a day? Watering Can is highest, Hoe is lowestHigh
Time savedHow much time does the upgrade save each day?High
Ability unlockedDoes it unlock new features (e.g., hardwood, iridium)?Medium
Opportunity costLoss from not having the tool for two daysLow
💡 Evaluation Example

Take the Copper Watering Can: watering 50 squares a day, the upgrade raises capacity from 40 to 55, saving one refill trip (about 2 in-game minutes). Two days without the can means hand-watering and losing energy. Overall: worth upgrading, and handing it over on a rainy day avoids the loss.

Tool upgrades require 5 bars of the corresponding ore. Below are the locations and efficiency for each ore:

✅ Ore Gathering Tips
  • Copper Ore is on levels 1–40. Prioritize copper-colored rocks; regular rocks may also drop copper.
  • Iron Ore concentrates on levels 41–80. Use staircases to fast-descend to level 50+ before mining.
  • Gold Ore is on levels 81–120, the deepest part of the regular Mines. Bring bombs to clear quickly.
  • Best iridium source: Skull Cavern with bombs + staircases to depth 50+, or defeat iridium slimes in the desert mine.
  • Watch TV fortune forecasts on Sundays — luck boosts mining drop rates.

⏰ Upgrade Timing Strategy

Timing significantly affects payoff. The key principle is "avoid busy farming periods, use the off-season":

  • Upgrade the Watering Can on rainy days: Rain waters crops automatically, so two days of can downtime cost nothing.
  • Upgrade all tools in Winter: No farming in Winter, so this is the perfect time to upgrade the Watering Can, Hoe, and Axe.
  • Don't upgrade the day before a festival: Clint doesn't work on festival days, delaying tool retrieval.
  • Upgrade at season transitions: e.g., late Spring to early Summer, when old crops are harvested and new ones not yet planted — minimal loss.
💡 Key Reminder

Tool upgrades are handed to Clint (the blacksmith). The handover day counts as day 1, and you can pick it up the next day — meaning "handover day + next day" is two days without the tool. Hand over in the morning, and do it on rainy days or in the off-season.

🤔 Tool Upgrades vs. Sprinklers: Which First?

This is the most common dilemma for new players: spend 2,000G + 5 Copper Bars to upgrade the Watering Can, or use the same materials to craft a sprinkler? Both solve the watering problem, but with different strategies:

Comparison Upgrade Watering Can Craft Sprinkler
Upfront cost2,000G + 5 Copper Bars1 Copper Bar + 1 Iron Bar (Basic) / 1 Gold Bar + 2 Iron Bars (Quality)
EffectLarger capacity, fewer refillsFully automated watering
Time takenStill need to water manuallyFrees you up — saves 1–2 hours a day
CoverageAll cropsBasic 1 square / Quality 3×3 / Iridium 5×5
Upgrade pathCan keep upgrading to IridiumNeed higher Farming level for better sprinklers
✅ Optimal Strategy

Early game (Farming levels 1–3): prioritize upgrading the Watering Can to Copper, since you can't craft Quality Sprinklers yet. Mid game (Farming levels 4–6): switch to crafting Quality Sprinklers (3×3 coverage), and upgrade the Watering Can to Steel as backup. Late game (Farming level 6+): lay out Iridium Sprinklers (5×5) everywhere; the Watering Can is only used for replanting and new plots.

⚔️ Weapon Acquisition Guide

Weapons come mainly from mine chests, the Adventurer's Guild, and monster drops:

  1. Rusty Sword: Starter weapon, only good for shallow mine levels.
  2. Steel Shortsword: Looted from a chest on Mine levels 1–5, an early transition weapon.
  3. Neptune's Glaive: From a chest around Mine level 20, main early weapon.
  4. Shadow Fang: Bought at the Adventurer's Guild (4,500G), main mid-game weapon.
  5. Onyx Blade: From a chest on Mine level 60+ or bought at the Guild.
  6. Galaxy Blade: Late-game Guild purchase (9,000G), recommended before heading to the desert.
  7. Lava Katana: From a chest near Mine level 80, the strongest in the regular Mines.
  8. Galaxy Sword: Offer a Prismatic Shard at the Three Pillars in the desert — one of the game's strongest swords.
💡 Getting the Galaxy Sword

Prismatic Shards come from breaking iridium nodes (deep in Skull Cavern) or as the first reward for donating to the museum. Bring a Prismatic Shard to the three pillars in the northwest corner of the desert to trigger the event and receive the Galaxy Sword. Get it as soon as you can reliably run Skull Cavern.

👜 Backpack Upgrade Importance

The backpack starts with only 12 slots, and upgrading is the most worthwhile early investment. Pierre's shop sells two upgrades:

  • First upgrade: 2,000G, expands to 24 slots. Essential early game.
  • Second upgrade: 10,000G, expands to 36 slots. Recommended mid-to-late game.
💡 Priority

The first backpack upgrade (2,000G) outranks most tool upgrades — a 24-slot backpack lets you carry more supplies while mining, more fish while fishing, and more loot while foraging. The indirect payoff far exceeds 2,000G. Buy it in Spring of Year 1.

📦 Storage Management Strategy

As the game progresses, the number of item types explodes, and without a good storage system you'll lose your mind searching. Recommended storage strategies:

  • Categorized storage: Each chest holds one category — minerals, crops, processed goods, weapons and gear, gifts, building materials.
  • Color coding: Use different chest colors for categories — red for minerals, green for crops, blue for fish, etc.
  • Place near use: Put a honey chest next to Bee Houses, a wine chest next to Kegs, an animal-product chest next to barns — cuts transport time.
  • Use the fridge: The kitchen fridge stores cooking ingredients. Keep ingredients there for easy cooking.
  • Sort within chests: Sort items inside each chest alphabetically or by value for easy lookup.
✅ Advanced Tip

Late game, build a "storage warehouse" at the center of the farm to centralize all chests. Connect functional areas with stone paths, and use a horse (Stable, 10,000G) or a Return Scepter for efficient logistics. The Mini-Chest added in 1.6 can be placed at remote spots (like the mine entrance or desert) as transit hubs.
