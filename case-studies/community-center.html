🏛️ Community Center Full Collection Route

The Community Center is one of Stardew Valley's core goals. Completing all bundles grants permanent rewards. This guide helps you plan the optimal route, listing all bundle item lists and acquisition locations by room, and providing season planning, rare item acquisition, missed-item recovery, and time estimation—a complete plan. The Community Center isn't a one-shot task—it spans all four seasons, requiring you to plan before each season begins which room to prepare what for; otherwise, missing once means waiting a year.

📋 Community Center Overview

The Community Center has 6 main rooms (Crafts Room, Pantry, Fish Tank, Boiler Room, Vault, Bulletin Board), and each room contains several bundle tasks. After completing all rooms' bundles, you'll receive:

  • Unlock the greenhouse (can plant any season's crops, year-round)
  • Repair the mine elevator (direct teleport to any unlocked floor)
  • Unlock the bus service (to the desert, to buy Starfruit seeds and other rare items)
  • Unlock the quarry (infinite source of stone, ore, and geodes)
  • Unlock the movie theater and Gunther storyline
  • Reward trophy and title, trigger the completion ending animation
💡 Important Note

The Community Center requires you to go to town after the 5th of the first spring to trigger the story (not on rainy days). If you enter Pelican Town for the first time after the 5th, you'll see Lewis and Morris's conversation, after which you can enter the Community Center to see the Junimo's needs. If you choose the Joja route, the Community Center becomes a Joja warehouse and the bundles cannot be completed.

🏠 Room-by-Room Guide

Each room has different difficulty and priority. We recommend advancing in the order "Crafts Room → Spring Crops Bundle → Fish Tank → Pantry → Boiler Room → Vault → Bulletin Board," completing easy early tasks first to build confidence.

🎨 Crafts Room

The Crafts Room contains the Spring Foraging Bundle, Summer Foraging Bundle, Fall Foraging Bundle, Winter Foraging Bundle, and Construction Bundle—5 bundles total. This is the Community Center's most time-consuming room because each season's foraging bundle must be completed in that season; missing it means waiting a year.

✅ Crafts Room Strategy

Reserve a "Community Center-dedicated field" (about 6×6 tiles) each season, exclusively for planting the crops needed for bundles. Note that the Quality Crops Bundle requires gold-quality crops, so pair with the Tiller branch (+10% quality probability) or use Quality Fertilizer.

🐟 Fish Tank

The Fish Tank contains four fish bundles, requiring you to collect fish from all seasons and locations. This is the room where beginners get stuck most easily—some fish have harsh appearance conditions (like the Legendary Fish only appearing on rainy spring days, or the Scorpion Carp only in the desert).

✅ Fish Tank Strategy

We recommend systematically fishing from the first spring, storing each new fish in the Community Center first. Prioritize rare fish on rainy days (catfish, eel, halibut), and fish for common ones normally to accumulate. The Legendary Fish is very difficult; we suggest reaching Fishing level 6 and using a trap bobber before attempting it.

📦 Pantry

The Pantry contains the Spring Crops Bundle, Summer Crops Bundle, Fall Crops Bundle, Animal Bundle, and Artisan Bundle—5 bundles total. It requires the combined operation of planting + animal husbandry + processing, making it the most comprehensive room.

🔥 Boiler Room

The Boiler Room contains the Blacksmith's Bundle, Geologist's Bundle, and Adventurer's Bundle, mainly requiring ore, gems, and monster drops. We recommend completing it during concentrated mining in the first winter.

💰 Vault

The Vault requires donating 500G, 2,500G, 5,000G, and 10,000G in succession—four gold donations total. This is the simplest room—as long as you have money, you can complete it without collecting items. We suggest donating all at once after the fall wine-making explosion.

📌 Bulletin Board

The Bulletin Board contains the Chef's Bundle, Field Research Bundle, Enchanter's Bundle, and Field Bundle. It requires comprehensive items from cooking, foraging, fishing, and combat, and is the last room completed.

📅 Recommended Completion Order

We suggest completing in the following order to maximize use of each season's resources and avoid repeatedly planting the same crop:

First Quarter (Spring)

🌱 Spring Crops Bundle

Location: Crafts Room. Requires 1 of each of 6 spring crops (gold quality):

  • Parsnip (grows 4 days, seed 20G)
  • Cauliflower (grows 12 days, seed 80G)
  • Potato (grows 6 days, seed 50G)
  • Green Bean (grows 10 days, multi-harvest, seed 40G)
  • Strawberry (grows 8 days, multi-harvest, seed 100G)
  • Rhubarb (grows 13 days, seed 100G, unlocked year 2)
🎣 Spring Fish Bundle

Location: Fish Tank. Requires 6 spring or year-round fish:

  • Trout (spring river, daytime)
  • Salmon (fall ocean, rainy)
  • Carp (year-round, mountain lake)
  • Chub (year-round, river)
  • Halibut (spring ocean, rainy)
  • Red Snapper (summer ocean, rainy)

Second Quarter (Summer)

☀️ Summer Crops Bundle

Location: Crafts Room. Requires 6 summer crops:

  • Melon (grows 12 days, seed 80G)
  • Blueberry (grows 13 days, multi-harvest, seed 80G)
  • Hot Pepper (grows 5 days, multi-harvest, seed 40G)
  • Hops (grows 11 days, multi-harvest, seed 60G)
  • Poppy (grows 7 days, seed 100G)
  • Sunflower (grows 8 days, seed 125G)
🌲 Foraging Bundle

Location: Crafts Room. Requires 12 wild foraged items:

  • Dandelion (spring), Wild Leek (spring), Daffodil (spring)
  • Raspberry (summer), Vanilla (summer)
  • Grape (fall), Mushroom (fall), Red Mushroom (fall), Blackberry (fall)
  • Crystal Fruit (winter), Holly (winter), Truffle (animal husbandry)

Third Quarter (Fall)

🍂 Fall Crops Bundle

Location: Crafts Room. Requires 5 fall crops:

  • Pumpkin (grows 13 days, seed 100G)
  • Cranberry (grows 7 days, multi-harvest, seed 240G)
  • Eggplant (grows 5 days, multi-harvest, seed 20G)
  • Yam (grows 10 days, seed 60G)
  • Pumpkin (gold quality) (Fall 15)
🐔 Animal Bundle

Location: Pantry. Requires 8 animal products or processed goods:

  • Egg (chicken), Milk (cow), Wool (sheep)
  • Duck Egg (duck), Goat Milk (goat)
  • Mayonnaise (processed), Cheese (processed), Cloth (processed)

Fourth Quarter (Winter + Cross-season)

⛏️ Minerals Bundle

Location: Boiler Room. Requires ore and gems:

  • Copper Bar, Iron Bar, Gold Bar, Iridium Bar (smelting)
  • Coal (mine), Quartz (mine)
  • Obsidian (deep mine), Earth Crystal (mine)
🍳 Special Items Bundle

Location: Bulletin Board. Requires rare items:

  • Ancient Fruit (rare planting), Prismatic Shard (very deep mine)
  • Void Essence (mine monsters), Golden Pumpkin (festival)
  • Gem (mine), Coffee (desert)

💎 Rare Item Acquisition Guide

Several Community Center items are very difficult; below are the key points for acquiring them:

  • Legendary Fish: Rainy spring days, mountain lake (on the wooden bridge north of JojaMart). Difficulty 110; we suggest Fishing level 6 + trap bobber + high-quality bait.
  • Scorpion Carp: Desert pond. Difficulty 90; requires unlocking the bus first (after the 10,000G Vault donation).
  • Prismatic Shard: Occasionally appears below mine floor 100, or in Skull Cavern chests. The most stable method is using the Galaxy Sword to strike crystal clusters deep in the mine.
  • Ancient Fruit: Trade an Ancient Seed with the desert trader, or find an Ancient Seed deep in the mine and plant it. Grows 28 days, harvests every 7 days.
  • Golden Pumpkin: Buy at the Spirit's Eve festival (Fall 27), or dig from mine chests.

🔄 Missed Item Recovery Plans

⏰ What if you permanently miss something?

If you missed an item from a certain season, don't worry! The same season will come again next year. The Community Center has no time limit—you can collect slowly. But several recovery strategies can speed things up:

  • Traveling Cart: Appears in the south of the forest every Friday and Sunday, randomly selling various items (including cross-season crops and fish). Prices are high, but you can buy needed items out of season. We suggest checking every week.
  • Seed Maker: Replicate Ancient Seeds in the Seed Maker, allowing out-of-season planting (requires greenhouse).
  • Desert Trader: Trade other items for rare crop seeds and fish.
  • Replant next year: The simplest method—replant the needed crop in the same season next year.
💡 Advance Preparation Advice
  • Start preparing the next season's needed crops before the season ends, to avoid scrambling at season transitions
  • Some items need cross-season collection (like fall salmon, which only appears in fall); check the calendar in advance
  • Rare items (like Prismatic Shards) require special conditions to obtain; plan the acquisition path in advance
  • Processed products require the corresponding machines (oil maker, Preserves Jar, keg, etc.); upgrade skills in advance
  • Reserve a "Community Center-dedicated field" each season for bundle crops, separate from money-making crops
