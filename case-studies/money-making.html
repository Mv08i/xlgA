💰 First-Year Million Guide

From 500G to breaking 1,000,000G before the end of the first year. This guide uses "fishing start → blueberry expansion → cranberry explosion" as the main line, giving specific strategies and target amounts for each month. This is a verified, reproducible route that doesn't rely on bugs or luck curves—only reasonable resource allocation and time management. The important thing is to understand why each stage is done this way—once you understand the logic of fund flow, you can flexibly adjust in your own run.

💡 Guide Prerequisites
  • Standard Farm start, reasonably plan time each day, averaging 1.5-2 hours of gameplay per day
  • Advance the Community Center main story (unlock the desert and greenhouse), but don't force completing it in the first year
  • Doesn't rely on bugs or cheats; achievable with normal play; requires some operational proficiency
  • Fishing skill requires practice—if fishing is your weak point, the target amounts may be delayed by one season
  • Based on version 1.6 data; some values in older versions (like Starfruit seed price) have been adjusted

🗺️ Overall Strategy Overview

The core logic of the first-year million is "fund compound interest"—every gold coin in the early game is invested in assets that produce more gold (sprinklers, kegs, animals) rather than hoarded or wasted. The year's funding curve grows exponentially: spring uses fishing to accumulate startup funds, summer uses blueberries to expand capacity, fall uses wine making to triple profit, and winter uses ore and remaining wine to maintain income and pave the way for year two.

The key to understanding this curve: don't chase book numbers in the early game—chase the accumulation of "productive assets." Having 10 kegs at the end of summer is more important than having 20,000G cash—because the former will keep producing in fall while the latter only depreciates.

🌸 Spring: Fishing Accumulation Period

Goal: Cumulative 30,000G + unlock the mine + copper Watering Can

Core Strategy

  • Days 1–5: Plant the initial 15 parsnips, spend the rest of the time fishing (ocean/forest lake)
  • Days 6–12: Upgrade to the fiberglass rod, fish catfish exclusively on rainy days (200G/each)
  • Days 11–13: Submit the copper Watering Can upgrade, taking advantage of rainy days so watering isn't delayed
  • Day 13: Attend the Egg Festival, find all 9 eggs in the Egg Hunt to win, and buy Strawberry seeds from Pierre
  • Days 14–24: Plant strawberries + keep fishing, save up to buy backpack upgrades
  • Days 24–28: After the Flower Dance, hoard funds for the big summer shopping spree
✅ Spring Key Point

Fishing is the most stable income source in the first year. On rainy days, catfish can earn 2,000G+ per day. Prioritize reaching Fishing level 5 to choose "Fisher," fish price +25%.

☀️ Summer: Planting Explosion Period

Goal: Cumulative 150,000G + quality sprinklers + unlock the desert

Core Strategy

  • Summer days 1–7: Plant all blueberries (80G/seed), summer's strongest multi-harvest crop
  • Summer Farming level 4: Unlock quality sprinklers at Farming level 4, lay them immediately
  • Summer days 8–14: Blueberries start harvesting, invest funds into building a coop and raising chickens
  • Summer days 15–21: Complete Community Center bus restoration, head to the desert to buy Starfruit seeds
  • Summer days 22–28: Replant Starfruit + hops, preparing for fall wine making
✅ Summer Key Point

One blueberry seed yields about 12 berries over the season (600G), making it summer's cost-effectiveness king. Also build the first batch of kegs as early as possible (Farming level 8), preparing for fall processing.

🍂 Fall: Profit Explosion Period

Goal: Cumulative 600,000G + multiple kegs running

Core Strategy

  • Fall days 1–7: Plant a pumpkin + cranberry combo, lay quality sprinklers
  • Fall days 8–14: Cranberries start harvesting, stable daily output
  • Fall days 15–21: Pumpkin harvest → kegs run in batch
  • Fall day 16: Stardew Valley Fair, display high-quality items to win star tokens for a Starfruit
  • Fall days 22–28: Expand keg scale, prepare for winter
✅ Fall Key Point

Pumpkin wine 720G/bottle, cranberry wine 450G/bottle—processing triples profit. This is the first year's most profitable season, bar none. Make sure to hoard enough kegs.

❄️ Winter: Passive Income Period

Goal: Cumulative 1,000,000G + unlock iridium ore

Core Strategy

  • Winter days 1–7: Concentrate on mining, push to floor 120, get Galaxy Sword materials
  • Winter days 8–14: After the Festival of Ice, head to the Skull Cavern to mine iridium
  • Winter days 15–21: Build a Deluxe Barn, start raising pigs (preparing for year two)
  • Winter days 22–28: Tidy up the farm, upgrade to iridium tools
  • Throughout: Winter fish + ore sales + remaining wine shipments maintain income
💡 Winter Income Sources

There are no crops in winter, but the wine hoarded from fall is still shipping, plus mine gems/geodes, winter fish, and Night Market goods—still maintaining 5,000G+ daily income. Also paves the way for the year-two pig-farming build.

🎯 Core Tips Summary

🎣 Fishing start

Spring fishing income in the first year far exceeds planting. Rainy-day catfish + Fisher branch, 2,000G+ per day is no dream.

🌱 Pick the right crops

Strawberry, blueberry, and cranberry—the three big multi-harvest crops—are the first year's profit pillars.

🏭 Wine making is king

Kegs + high-value crops (Starfruit, pumpkin, Ancient Fruit) are the key to late-game gold explosion.

⏰ Time management

Water every morning → fish/mine in the afternoon → process and ship at night, maximizing time use.

✅ Mindset Advice

A million isn't a hard target—enjoying the process matters more than hitting the number. If your rhythm is a bit slower, that's fine too; in year two, pig farming and Ancient Fruit wine will grow your wealth exponentially.

🎣 Fishing Income Deep Strategy

Fishing is spring's most stable income source in the first year, far exceeding planting. Mastering the details below can push your spring daily average income to 2,000G+:

  • Equipment upgrade path: Bamboo pole (gift from Willy) → Fiberglass rod (buy for 1,800G after Spring day 6) → Iridium rod (buy for 7,500G in late spring). Each upgrade significantly boosts fishing efficiency.
  • Bait and tackle: Buy bait immediately after unlocking the fiberglass rod (5G each), bite speed +50%. The iridium rod can equip tackle; the trap bobber keeps hard-to-catch fish from escaping.
  • Target fish selection: On rainy spring days fish catfish exclusively (200G/each, mountain river); normally fish ocean fish like halibut and eel; in summer catch octopus and red snapper.
  • Treasure chest income: After Fishing level 6, treasure chest appearance rate +25%. Chests often contain ore, gems, and rare items (like Artifact Spots)—a hidden income source.
  • Skill branches: Choose "Fisher" at level 5 (fish price +25%), then "Angler" at level 10 (fish price +50%). Note that Angler replaces Fisher rather than stacking with it, so the total bonus is +50% (not +75%)—a single catfish can sell for 300G (200G base × 1.5).
✅ Fishing Daily Income Reference

Rainy spring days catching catfish: 2,500-3,500G/day; sunny spring days catching ocean fish: 1,500-2,000G/day; rainy summer days catching octopus: 3,000G+/day. Paired with the Fisher branch, a whole season of fishing income can reach 20,000-30,000G.

⛏️ Mine Income Strategy

The mine isn't just the source of tool upgrades and sprinklers—it's also stable income itself. In winter, with no crops, the mine is one of the main income sources:

  • Geode income: Geode nodes appear about every 5 floors. Opening geodes yields ore, gems, and rare minerals (like Earth Crystal, Fire Quartz). A regular geode costs 50G to open, but the gems inside are worth 100-300G.
  • Gem sales: Sapphire, ruby, emerald, topaz, aquamarine—each worth 150-250G. Diamonds appear below floor 80, each worth 750G.
  • Ore sales: Gold bar 250G, iridium bar 1,000G. Don't sell early—save for tool upgrades; later, when iridium ore is surplus, you can sell.
  • Skull Cavern: After unlocking the desert, you can enter. Iridium ore appears in large quantities. When pushing floors in winter, daily iridium income can reach 5,000-10,000G.
  • Coal and copper: Don't sell early; later, when coal is surplus, you can sell in bulk (coal 15G/piece).

💡 Automation Rhythm Advice
  • Early summer: 5-10 quality sprinklers, covering 40-80 tiles of planting area, saving 1 hour of watering time per day
  • Early fall: 15-20 quality sprinklers, covering 120-160 tiles, basically fully automating the planting area
  • Late fall / winter: Start crafting iridium sprinklers, preparing for large-scale expansion in year two
  • Sprinklers need scarecrow protection, or crows will eat your crops

🐔 Animal Husbandry Investment Timing

Animal husbandry is the main income in year two, but the first year also needs moderate investment to start the processing chain:

  • Mid-late summer: Build the first coop (4,000G + wood 300 + stone 100), raise 4 chickens. Start producing eggs, make mayonnaise.
  • Early fall: Upgrade to a deluxe coop, build the first barn (6,000G + wood 350 + stone 150), raise cows for milk.
  • Late fall: Start crafting cheese presses, processing milk into cheese (cheese sells for 200G, milk 125G).
  • Winter: Build a deluxe barn, preparing for year-two pig farming. Pigs are year two's most profitable animal (truffle 625G each, 1-2 per day).
✅ Animal Husbandry Income Reference

First-year animal husbandry is mainly for starting the processing chain; pure profit isn't high (daily average 500-1,000G). The real explosion is in year two—a full pen of 12 pigs produces 7,500G+ of truffles per day, and paired with the Artisan branch (processed goods +40%) can reach 10,000G+ daily.
