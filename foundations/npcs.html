👥 Villager Relationship System

Pelican Town is home to more than 30 villagers with distinct personalities. Building friendships with them unlocks storylines, letters, recipes, and even marriage. The villager relationship system is one of Stardew Valley's most enduring features—every NPC has a unique backstory, daily schedule, and gift preferences. This guide covers friendship mechanics in detail, all marriage candidates, heart event trigger conditions, the marriage system, and practical methods for efficiently maxing out friendship.

💛 Friendship System In-Depth

Each villager's friendship is shown intuitively in "hearts." Regular villagers cap at 8 hearts, and marriage candidates cap at 10 hearts (14 after marriage). Understanding the underlying mechanics of friendship lets you plan your social strategy efficiently.

  • Each heart = 250 friendship points; a regular villager at max hearts needs 2000 points, a marriage candidate needs 2500
  • First daily conversation: +20 points (regular villagers), +10 points (marriage candidates). Note: talking again on the same day gives no further points
  • Gift frequency: Max 2 gifts per person per day, and max 2 per week. The weekly counter resets on Monday morning
  • Birthday gifts: Friendship gain ×8—a key moment for pushing heart levels. A loved gift on a birthday equals 8 normal gifts
  • Festival encounters: Talking to NPCs at festivals grants a small friendship bonus
  • Friendship decay: If you go long without interaction, friendship drops a little each day (about 2 points)—occasional dialogue or gifts maintain it
  • Marriage is gender-neutral: Any gender can pursue any of the 12 candidates; the game is LGBTQ+ friendly
💡 Friendship Decay Rule

If you go long without interacting with a villager, friendship drops by about 2 points per day (except for your spouse). This means even if you forget about a villager for months, friendship only declines slowly and won't be wiped out. Periodic gifts or conversations maintain it—no need to worry about major regression. But if you want to keep max hearts, interact at least once a week.

🎁 Gift-Giving Mechanics & Strategy

Gifting is the fastest way to boost friendship. Each villager rates every item on a five-tier scale: "Loved / Liked / Neutral / Disliked / Hated." The rating appears in the item hover info (after you've given that item at least once). Below is the detailed bonus table:

Rating Normal Friendship Birthday Friendship Notes
Loved+80+640 (×8)Top gift choice, most efficient
Liked+45+360Second-best—use when you can't find a loved gift
Neutral+20+160Barely usable when there's no special preference
Disliked-20-160Loses points—avoid giving these
Hated-40-320Heavy penalty—a wrong birthday gift is devastating
✅ Efficient Gifting Strategy
  • Max 2 per week—always give "loved" gifts; it's more efficient than 4 liked gifts
  • A loved gift on the birthday equals 8 normal gifts—the calendar is a must-read; missing a birthday is a huge loss
  • "Universal loves": Rabbit's Foot (from raising rabbits), Pearl—almost every villager loves them
  • "Universal likes": All flowers (except Crocus), Coffee, Maple Syrup, Fruit Wine, etc.—useful as backups
  • Try once first: After giving an item, its rating shows up—useful for planning future gifts
  • Check the map before gifting: Confirm the NPC is home to avoid a wasted trip

❤️ Complete Heart Event Guide

Reaching certain heart levels triggers that villager's exclusive story event, mostly at specific locations and times (like entering their home, specific seasons, etc.). Heart events are the core way to understand an NPC's background and personality. Some events have dialogue options that affect subsequent friendship.

✅ Tips for Triggering Heart Events
  • Story events usually require entering the NPC's room (like their home) with the NPC present
  • Check the map to confirm their location before heading over—avoid wasted trips
  • Some events require specific weather (like sunny) or season—check the NPC wiki for details
  • Some 10-heart events must trigger on rainy days or at specific times—plan ahead
  • Wrong dialogue choices give less friendship but don't permanently affect the story—you can reload and retry

💒 Complete Marriage Process

Marriage is one of the core goals of Stardew Valley's villager relationships. After marriage, your spouse moves into the farmhouse, helps with chores, gives gifts, and provides emotional companionship. Here's the complete process:

  1. Reach 8 hearts: Through gifts and conversation, raise your favorite candidate's friendship to 8 hearts—this is the prerequisite for proposing
  2. Buy a Mermaid's Pendant: On a rainy day (non-Winter), go to the bridge at the beach and buy a Mermaid's Pendant from the Old Mariner for 5,000G. He only appears on rainy days
  3. Give the pendant to propose: Give the Mermaid's Pendant to a marriage candidate at 8+ hearts—they'll accept and you'll be engaged
  4. Wedding 3 days later: The wedding ceremony happens automatically on the morning of the 3rd day after engagement; your spouse officially moves into the farmhouse
  5. Married life: Your spouse helps water crops, feed animals, make breakfast, and occasionally gives you gifts. Raising friendship further unlocks 14-heart events
💡 Marriage Essentials
  • After marriage, the spouse's friendship cap rises to 14 hearts (3500 points)
  • Your spouse helps with different chores based on personality (e.g., Abigail feeds animals, Penny waters crops)
  • To divorce, apply to Mayor Lewis—it costs 50,000G, and friendship resets to zero after divorce
  • Your spouse won't wander far from the farmhouse, but you can still check their mood and talk in the Social tab
  • In multiplayer, you can't marry the same NPC—coordinate who pursues whom

👥 Other Important NPCs & Functions

Beyond marriage candidates, Pelican Town has many functional NPCs who provide shops, services, and quests—indispensable as you progress through the game:

  • Mayor Lewis: Posts quests, collects taxes, at the Community Center entrance. Hosts the Feast of the Winter Star each year
  • Wizard Rasmodius: Guides Community Center restoration; provides the mirror for free appearance changes. Located in the Wizard's Tower in the western forest
  • Marnie: Animal vendor—a must-visit for raising chickens and cows. Located at Marnie's Ranch; lives with Shane and Jas
  • Robin: Carpenter—see her for all building upgrades. Located at the Carpenter's Shop; mother of Maru and Sebastian
  • Clint: Blacksmith—tool upgrades and cracking open geodes. Located at the Blacksmith; has a crush on Emily
  • Willy: Fishing gear vendor—fishing rod upgrades. Located at the Fish Shop on the beach; buys fish daily
  • Pierre: Grocer—seeds and backpack upgrades. Shop closed on Tuesdays and Sundays
  • Traveling Cart (Merchant): Wandering merchant who sells rare seeds and items in the forest on Fridays and Sundays
  • Old Mariner: Mysterious figure who appears at the beach bridge on rainy days; sells the Mermaid's Pendant
  • Sandy: Desert shop owner; sells Desert-exclusive seeds

📍 Villager Schedules & Finding Tips

Every NPC has a fixed schedule that changes based on day of the week, season, and weather. Mastering their patterns dramatically boosts gifting efficiency:

Afternoon 12:00–17:00Town square / river / shopsSocial time—NPCs scatter around town

✅ Efficient Friendship Grinding
  • Concentrated evening gifting: 7:00–10:00 PM, most NPCs are at the Stardrop Saloon—you can gift 5–8 in one trip
  • Always gift on birthdays: Mark all birthdays on the calendar—a loved birthday gift equals 8 normal gifts
  • Festival dialogue: Talk to every NPC at festivals for free friendship bonuses
  • Adjust for rain: Some NPCs stay home on rainy days—easier to find
  • Help-wanted board quests: Complete NPCs' help-wanted board quests for friendship bonuses
  • Stockpile universal gifts: Hoard coffee, flowers, and other universally liked items for NPCs you haven't studied in depth
💡 Long-Term Returns on Friendship

Villagers with high friendship will send recipes, seeds, and rare items to your mailbox (like Linus's Survival Burger recipe, Robin's furniture recipe). Some NPCs also lower shop prices or unlock special storylines (like Gus's saloon discount). Social investment pays off long-term—aim to push major NPCs past 8 hearts in year one.
