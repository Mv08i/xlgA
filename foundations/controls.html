🎮 Controls Guide

Familiarity with key bindings and the interface can double your farm efficiency. Stardew Valley's controls seem simple, but many players discover hidden shortcut features only after hundreds of hours. This guide provides a complete list of keyboard shortcuts, controller layout, interface element explanations, useful tips, and advanced methods for inventory management and tool switching—helping you evolve from "functional" to "efficient."

✅ Keyboard Efficiency Secrets
  • Put your most-used tools in the first few hotbar slots (1–5); switching with number keys is much faster than the scroll wheel
  • When shopping, hold Shift to buy 5 at a time, or Ctrl to buy a full stack—no need to click repeatedly

🖥️ Complete Game Interface Breakdown

Stardew Valley's interface has high information density—understanding each element is the prerequisite for efficient operation. Here are detailed explanations of all key elements on the main interface:

⚡ Energy Bar (bottom-left)

Green progress bar, starting at 270 points. When energy runs out, you can't use tools (but can still move and pick items up); eat food to restore. Below 50% energy, the character pants as a warning.

❤️ Health (bottom-left)

Red heart-shaped bar, only shown in the mines or combat scenes. Hitting zero makes you pass out and lose gold (about 10%) and some items. Hidden and unused outside combat.

🕐 Time Clock (top-right)

From 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM the next day; at 2:00 AM you pass out and lose gold. Sleeping after 12:00 AM doesn't fully restore energy. Time pauses indoors or in menus—use this to organize your inventory.

💰 Gold Display (top-right)

Currently held gold. Items shipped at the shipping bin settle into your account at 6:00 AM the next day—they don't show immediately. Press G to enlarge the display.

🎒 Hotbar (bottom of screen)

12–36 slots; use number keys to switch the current tool slot. After a backpack upgrade you can flip rows (LT/RT or -/=). The currently selected slot is highlighted.

📅 Calendar (Pierre's General Store)

Shows festivals and NPC birthdays—key for planning gifts and stock in advance. You can also buy a calendar furniture item from Robin to place on your farm.

📋 Quest Log (press J or in menu)

View current quests, requests, and help-wanted board tasks. Completing them auto-settles rewards; expired quests are marked as failed.

🗺️ Map (press M)

Shows all of Pelican Town, your farm, and mine progress. Colored head markers show NPC locations; the green arrow is you. Quickly locate villagers.

📊 Character Menu In-Depth (press E to open)

The character menu has multiple tabs, each with important functions:

  • Inventory tab: Equipment slots (tool, 2 rings, hat, boots, shirt, pants), backpack grid, organize button, current buff display
  • Skills tab: Shows the five skills' levels and experience progress bars; displays profession choices after level 10
  • Social tab: All NPCs' friendship hearts, whether you've given a gift today/this week, and marriage status
  • Map tab: Same as pressing M—view the world map and NPC locations
  • Collections tab: Items, fish, recipes, and letters you've collected—indicates completion progress
  • Exit tab: Save and exit, adjust options, view achievements

⚡ Useful Shortcut Features Compendium

Many players don't discover these shortcut features even after hundreds of hours. Mastering them dramatically boosts efficiency:

  • Quick place: Hold an item (sprinkler, seed, furniture) and click the ground to place it directly—no need to open the menu
  • Bulk buy: In shops, hold Shift and click an item to buy 5 at a time; hold Ctrl to buy a full backpack
  • Sell all: At the shipping bin, hold Shift and click an item to sell all of that type—no need to drag one by one
  • Quick return home: After repairing the minecart, you can spend a small amount of gold to fast-travel between the mines, forest, and farm
  • Horse speed boost: Riding a horse increases movement speed by 30%; ride to the mine entrance before descending to save time
  • Check friendship: Press E and open the Social tab to view all NPCs' friendship and whether you've gifted today
  • NPC map markers: Press M to open the map—colored head markers show NPC locations, essential for gift-giving
  • TV programs: Watch TV daily for the weather forecast, Queen of Sauce recipes, and luck forecasts—a must
  • Mailbox shortcuts: Right-click items attached to letters to send them straight to your inventory—no need to drag
  • Chest quick access: Open a chest and hold Shift while clicking items to deposit/withdraw entire stacks
💡 Advanced Operation Tips
  • Holding Shift while shipping sells all of that item at once—no need to click repeatedly
  • After upgrading tools, charging lets you process multiple tiles (e.g., Iridium Hoe tills 6 tiles, Iridium Watering Can waters 18)
  • Put your most-used tool (like the Watering Can) in slot 1; press 1 to switch fast
  • When surrounded in combat, quickly switch to your sword and right-click to block your way out
  • Time pauses when you open a menu indoors—organize your inventory at the mine entrance without losing time
  • When fishing, hold right-click to reel in, release to pause—mastering the rhythm is the key to becoming a fishing pro

🎒 Inventory Space Management Tips

Backpack space is one of the most scarce resources in the early game. The 12-slot starting backpack fills up fast when mining or foraging. Here's how to manage your inventory efficiently:

  • Prioritize backpack upgrades: Save up 2000G and immediately upgrade to 24 slots at Pierre's General Store; 10000G gets you 36 slots. This is the most worthwhile early investment.
  • Smart zoning: Divide your backpack into zones—the first 6 slots for tools (Watering Can, hoe, axe, Pickaxe, sword, fishing rod), middle for seeds and food, back for harvested goods.
  • Use chests liberally: Place chests at the farm and mine entrance to categorize items (crop chest, mineral chest, resource chest). A chest only costs 50 wood—very cheap.
  • Stack same-type items: Regularly organize your backpack and stack same-type items (up to 999/slot). Right-click drag to split, left-click drag to merge.
  • Empty before leaving: Before going to the mines or fishing, store unneeded items in chests—bring only essential tools and food.
  • Use the Recycling Machine: Trash can be put into the Recycling Machine to become useful materials—reduces waste from discarding.

🔧 Tool Switching & Usage Tips

Tool-switching efficiency directly impacts operation fluidity. Master these tips to switch seamlessly between farming, combat, and foraging:

📌 Fix tool positions

Put tools in fixed positions (e.g., 1=Watering Can, 2=Hoe, 3=Axe, 4=Pickaxe, 5=Sword, 6=Fishing Rod)—muscle memory makes switching extremely fast.

⚡ Number keys beat the wheel

Number keys jump directly to the target slot; the wheel cycles one at a time. Once you're used to number keys, efficiency is far higher.

🔄 Charge release timing

After upgrading tools, hold left-click to charge and release to fire. Copper charges in about 1 second, Iridium about 2 seconds—anticipate the timing.

⚔️ Combat tool switching

In the mines, put your sword in slot 1; when swarmed by monsters, press 1 to switch, right-click to block, left-click to attack—faster reactions.

🔧 Custom Key Bindings & Settings

The game supports customizing most keys in "Settings → Control Settings"—adjust to your personal preference. You can also adjust sensitivity, rumble on/off, UI scaling, and other options. Beginners should try the defaults first and adjust only what feels off. Some settings worth adjusting:

  • UI scaling: On high-resolution screens, enlarge the UI so text isn't hard to read
  • Rumble intensity: Controller players can adjust or turn off rumble
  • Auto-run: On by default; turn off to require holding a key to run
  • Tool aiming: When enabled, shows the tool's affected area—great for planning
  • Dialogue speed: Speed up dialogue scrolling to save time
