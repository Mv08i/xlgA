📖 Usage Notes

  • Profit per plant = (sale price × number of harvests) - seed price
  • Total profit = profit per plant × plant count
  • Daily profit = profit per plant / growth cycle
  • Energy efficiency = profit per plant / (watering energy × growth days)
💡 Tips

In actual play, the advanced Farming skill (Tiller branch) gives a 10% sale price bonus. Quality Sprinklers can save watering energy.
